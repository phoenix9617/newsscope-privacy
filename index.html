Privacy Policy for NewsScope

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NewsScope ("we", "us", or "our") handles information collected through our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

NewsScope is designed as an open news aggregator. We do not require personal accounts or collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or physical address.

  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Automated technical data such as device model, OS version, and network state may be transmitted to optimize content delivery and app stability.
  • Advertising Data: We display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), general IP-based location, and interaction events to deliver relevant advertising.

2. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device. Please review their privacy practices:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by our app and partner SDKs is used strictly to:

  • Maintain and operate the news stream and content ranking features.
  • Monitor, diagnose, and resolve technical bugs and crashes.
  • Serve non-intrusive in-feed sponsored content via Google AdMob.

4. Data Retention and Security

We do not store user profiles on external servers. All network communications between NewsScope and backend services are encrypted in transit via industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Children's Privacy

NewsScope is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
