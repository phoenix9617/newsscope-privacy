Account & Data Deletion Request

App: NewsScope | Developer: New Reach Venture

If you have registered an account to comment or vote in NewsScope, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to support@newreachventure.com from the email address linked to your NewsScope account.

Step 2: Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - NewsScope.

Step 3: State your username in the body of the message to help us verify ownership.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User authentication record (email address and hashed password credentials).
  • Public profile data (display name and avatar selections).
  • Comment author attribution (comments will either be removed or fully anonymized).

Data Retention and Processing Time

  • Account records and associated identifiers are processed and permanently removed within 30 days of verification.
  • Aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics (such as total anonymous vote counts or aggregated app crash diagnostics) may be retained for system stability.
